      Settings

      Changes are saved automatically.

      Playback

      s

      When you press play after pausing, the music fades in over this many seconds instead of coming on at full volume. 0 turns it off.

      Plays everything in mono, so neither ear misses part of a song. Useful with one earbud in, or if you hear better on one side. A change applies at once.

      When the last song of the playlist ends, it starts again from the first song instead of stopping.

      Tracks

      A track that is not included is left out of the track list, and tracks without synced lyrics are skipped when playing. Tracks without audio can never be played; this only shows or hides them. A change applies at once.

      Page

      Turn off what you don't need on this page to make it lighter on your computer. With both off, the page rests until you play, pause or change song. This only changes this page, not the lyrics shown on top of other windows (that is the Overlay, below). Your keyboard's play and pause keys keep working either way, even with this page closed. To fix lyrics that are too early or too late, turn Show lyrics on: the timing buttons are above the lyrics.

      Keyboard

      These keys work even while a game or another window is in front, and with this page closed. To use different keys for the volume, change them in your desktop's keyboard shortcut settings.

      Overlay

      overlay not running

      The overlay shows the line being sung on top of everything else, even a full-screen game. Turn it off if you only want the music: it is closed completely, so it uses nothing until you turn it back on. Your overlay settings below are kept either way.
